# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct errors openly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private data, or private correspondence.
- Avoid overstating research results or presenting benchmark screens as scientific proof.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, or retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact mechanism in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical and will explain enforcement decisions to affected participants.
